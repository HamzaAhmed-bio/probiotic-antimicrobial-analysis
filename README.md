# probiotic-antimicrobial-analysis

Undergraduate capstone project (Ain Shams University, 2025) evaluating the antimicrobial activity of probiotic bacterial strains, combining laboratory assay data with genomic sequence analysis.

This repository contains the data analysis side of the project: R scripts used to process growth and inhibition-zone measurements, the statistical comparison across strains, and the figures produced for the final report.

## Background

Probiotic strains can inhibit the growth of pathogenic bacteria, and the mechanisms are partly attributable to identifiable genes and gene clusters. This project tested a panel of strains for antimicrobial activity in the lab and looked for corresponding genomic features in their sequences.

## Methods

- **Laboratory:** bacterial culture, growth measurement, and inhibition-zone assays across the strain panel
- **Sequence analysis:** alignment and functional annotation of bacterial genomic sequences to identify genes and gene clusters associated with antimicrobial activity
- **Statistics:** comparison of antimicrobial activity across strains in R

## Requirements

R 4.2+ with `ggplot2`, `dplyr`, `tidyr`.
